# Code of Conduct

## Our Promise

We promise to make this project a safe and welcoming place for everyone, no
matter their age, body size, disability, race, gender, religion, or identity.

We promise to act in ways that build an open, friendly, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

**Expected behavior:**

- Be kind and understanding
- Respect different opinions and experiences
- Give and accept helpful feedback
- Own your mistakes, apologize, and learn
- Think about what's best for the whole community

**Unacceptable behavior:**

- Sexual language, images, or unwanted attention
- Trolling, insults, or personal attacks
- Harassment in public or private
- Sharing private information without permission
- Anything else that doesn't belong in a professional space

## Our Responsibility

Project leaders will:

- Explain what good behavior looks like
- Take fair action when someone breaks the rules
- Remove, edit, or reject comments, code, and issues that don't follow this policy
- Explain why they made a moderation decision

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces (GitHub, Discord, etc.) and when representing the project in public.

## Reporting

If someone is being abusive or harassing you, report it privately by emailing
[the maintainer](mailto:daniel.rosemann10@gmail.com). We will review every
report quickly and fairly. Reports submitted through this email channel will
be kept confidential.
