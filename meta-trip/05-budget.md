# Budget: What the Trip Costs

Per person, 4 days from Germany. Three levels; mid is the itinerary
as written.

## Flights

| Level | Return fare Germany → SFO |
|---|---|
| Budget | ~€450 (1-stop, shoulder season) |
| Mid | ~€650 (direct or good 1-stop) |
| Comfort | ~€900+ (direct, peak dates) |

## On the ground (4 days)

| Item | Budget | Mid | Comfort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hotel (3 nights, +~14% tax) | ~€300 (private room) | ~€550 (citizenM/Lucent class) | ~€900+ (Park James/Nia) |
| SFO transfers (BART+Caltrain ×2) | ~€22 | ~€22 (+1 rideshare ~€55) | ~€130 (rideshares) |
| Local transit (Caltrain/bus) | ~€40 | ~€60 | ~€60 |
| Day-2 organized tour | ~€120 (group tour) | ~€180 (small-group/full-day) | ~€1,500 (private tour) |
| Museum entries | ~€25 | ~€25 | ~€25 |
| Food (€45/75/120 per day) | ~€180 | ~€300 | ~€480 |
| ESTA (amortized, 2-yr validity) | ~€19 | ~€19 | ~€19 |
| Buffer/misc | ~€100 | ~€150 | ~€250 |

## Totals (flight + ground, per person)

- **Budget: ~€1,250–€1,500**
- **Mid: ~€1,800–€2,200**
- **Comfort/private-tour: ~€3,500–€4,500**

## Money-saving levers

1. September/October shoulder season for fares + hotels
2. BART+Caltrain instead of rideshares (saves ~€100)
3. Group tour instead of private (saves 10× on Day 2)
4. Private-room stays vs hotels (saves ~€250)
5. ESTA lasts 2 years — the second trip skips the fee
