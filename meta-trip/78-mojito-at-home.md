# 78 — Mojito at Home (Bonus Fun)

The ritual, recreated in Hanau. Not the real Philz, close enough to dream.

## Ingredients, one glass

- Strong coffee, chilled. Fresh mint, bruised not torn. Ice, plenty.
- Sweetness to taste, lime wedge, cold water or sparkle.

## Method

- Muddle mint gently with sweetness. Add ice, coffee, water. Stir once.
- Garnish, photograph, drink while paging the album.

## Rules

- Morning only, caffeine half-life 6 h per [23-caffeine-sleep](23-caffeine-sleep.md).
- Second glass is a postcard to California, not a plan. One is ritual, two is jet lag cosplay.
