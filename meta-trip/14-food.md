# Food: Meal by Meal

Every meal of the 4 days, named places with backups. Prices are per
person, 2026 levels, verified from current guides — re-check hours
the week before, restaurants close and move.

## Outbound morning (Hanau + FRA)

- **05:30 home:** light breakfast only (yogurt/muesli, coffee) —
  the real breakfast is at the gate. Heavy food + 11.5 h cabin = regret.
- **08:40 gate (FRA T1):** proper second breakfast. T1 airside has
  bakeries and coffee bars past security; eat sandwiches, not just
  croissants — lunch is 1 h after takeoff but small. Budget ~€10–15.
- **Fill the water bottle** at the fountain after security.

## In the air (LH454)

- **~11:10 CET lunch** (~1 h after takeoff): eat everything, it's
  the best meal of the flight. Water with it, no second coffee yet.
- **Mid-flight snack round:** take it, even if not hungry — blood
  sugar on landing decides your evening.
- **~19:00 CET breakfast** (~90 min before landing): eat, then teeth,
  then no more food until Menlo Park. Coffee only now if you must
  (it's ~10:00 SFO time — correct timezone for caffeine).

## Day 0 dinner — Menlo Park, Santa Cruz Ave (~18:30)

Pick one primary, keep one backup (jet lag kills decision-making):

- **Primary: yeobo, darling** (827 Santa Cruz Ave) — Korean-Taiwanese
  from the Maum (Michelin-star) couple, Chronicle Top-100 2026.
  Comfort food that lands well when tired. ~$40–60.
- **Backup: Camper** (898 Santa Cruz Ave) — Michelin-recommended
  California, sunlit corner room, pork ragu rigatoni. ~$40–60.
- **Cheap/tired option:** pizza slice or tacos on the Ave, ~$15–20,
  then bed. No shame — Day 1 is the payoff.
- Water with dinner, no alcohol on night one (jet lag multiplier).

## Day 1 (tour day)

- **Breakfast:** hotel or **Mademoiselle Colette** (French bakery,
  Santa Cruz Ave) — croissant + coffee to go, ~$10.
- **Lunch:** tour-dependent (confirm included vs own at booking).
  If own: Stanford area or museum cafe, ~$15–20.
- **Dinner:** **Eylan** (500 El Camino Real, Indian, Michelin Bib
  Gourmand 2026) or **Bistro Vida** (641 Santa Cruz Ave, full bar).
  ~$40–60. This is the celebration dinner — the sign is done.

## Day 2 (self-guided)

- **Breakfast:** Cafe Borrone (1010 El Camino Real) — the local
  anchor, patio, ~$12. Or hotel.
- **Lunch:** Computer History Museum cafe (fast, no transit loss)
  or Castro Street, Mountain View (10 min walk, many options),
  ~$15–20.
- **Dinner:** University Ave, Palo Alto — **Local Union 271**
  (pizza + local beers, patio) or deli at New York New York
  Sandwiches for a cheap classic. ~$20–40.

## Day 3 (Apple + departure)

- **Breakfast:** hotel checkout + **Philz Coffee** run (Bay Area
  ritual — order the Mint Mojito iced coffee). ~$7.
- **Lunch:** **Apple Park Visitor Center cafe** (coffee with a
  spaceship view) + HP-garage-area bite in Palo Alto. ~$15–25.
- **SFO dinner:** International Terminal G — **1300 on Fillmore**
  (sit-down) or **Mustards Bar & Grill**; fast option **Gott's
  Roadside** (Intl A) / **Bun Mee** (Vietnamese sandwiches). ~$20–35.
- **FRA return:** pretzel + coffee at arrival, real meal in Hanau.

## Staples, groceries, coffee strategy

- **Grocery run Day 0 or 1:** Trader Joe's (snacks, fruit, water
  for Caltrain rides and Bayfront picnic). ~$20 covers the trip.
- **Coffee rule:** Philz once (experience), hotel/Pete's otherwise.
  Airport coffee is $6+ — fill before security, buy after.
- **Water rule:** tap is safe everywhere; one bottle refilled all
  trip. Dehydration magnifies jet lag more than any food choice.
- **Total food budget:** ~€180 budget / ~€300 mid / ~€480 comfort
  (matches [05-budget.md](05-budget.md)) — mid means one $50 dinner
  (Day 1), $25 lunches, $10–15 breakfasts.
