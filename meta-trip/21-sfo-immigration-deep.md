# 21 — SFO Immigration Deep Dive

Companion to [09-the-flight](09-the-flight.md) landing section and [12-timeline-arrival](12-timeline-arrival.md). CBP is the biggest variance block of the whole trip: 15 to 60 min.

## Before the hall

- Follow Arrivals and Baggage Claim signs down. Do not stop for coffee.
- Passport open at photo page. Phone with return ticket and hotel address ready.
- Script: tourism, four days, hotel in Menlo Park, flying home Saturday.

## The lines

- Join ESTA and Visa Waiver visitors line. Not US citizens, not crew.
- Wide-bodies landing together means queues spike. One officer per 40 people is normal.
- Photo plus fingerprints taken. Answer short, match your ESTA.

## Bags and customs

- Screens show belt like CL1. Collect, verify tag.
- Trolley if needed, small fee. Keep passport pouch zipped.
- Walk through nothing-to-declare exit. Spot checks are rare. Food and plant products are the classic trap, so declare them.
- Landside around 13:00 local, 22:00 Hanau time. Rule: no sitting still longer than train rides until 21:00.

## If flagged to secondary

- Stay calm. It is usually a document recheck, not an accusation.
- Show return ticket, hotel booking, and ESTA approval screenshot.
- Do not joke, do not volunteer extra trips. Short answers win.

## Checklist

- [ ] Passport photo page open
- [ ] Return ticket on phone
- [ ] Hotel address pinned
- [ ] Water after hall, not before
