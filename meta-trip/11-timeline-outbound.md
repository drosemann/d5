# Minute Timeline: Hanau Door → FRA Gate (Outbound Morning)

Reference: LH454 dep 10:10, T1 Z gate. All times CEST. Rule: if anything
slips >10 min, skip the gate-breakfast block first, never the buffers.

## 05:30–06:00 — Wake + out the door (30 min)

- 05:30 Alarm. Phone off airplane mode, check DB Navigator for S8/S9
  disruptions Hanau→Frankfurt. No disruption → proceed. Disruption →
  fallback: RE/RB to Frankfurt Hbf + S8/S9 to airport (adds ~20 min,
  still fine), or taxi to Hanau Hbf to catch an earlier S-Bahn.
- 05:32–05:42 Wash, dress (travel outfit + fleece accessible on top
  of bag), contact lenses/glasses, teeth.
- 05:42–05:52 Final sweep, room by room: passport pouch (passport,
  ESTA screenshot, return ticket, hotel address, insurance), phone,
  power bank, headphones, eye mask, earplugs, pen, snacks, water
  bottle (empty — fill after security), chargers, fleece.
- 05:52–05:55 Photos of passport/ESTA already on phone? Verify.
  Boarding pass in Lufthansa app? Verify. Weigh bag mentally (<8 kg
  cabin if no checked bag).
- 05:55–06:00 Shoes on, doors locked, leave. Buffer: 10 min early
  is the goal; 06:05 latest.

## 06:00–06:30 — Home → Hanau Hbf → platform (30 min)

- 06:00–06:12 Transit to Hanau Hbf (bus/tram per local schedule;
  taxi ~€10 if connections are thin — decide the night before).
- 06:12–06:18 Buy **Hanau → Frankfurt Flughafen Regionalbahnhof**
  single (~€7.80) in RMV app (preferred — timestamped, no stamping)
  or machine. Confirm destination reads "Regionalbahnhof", not Hbf.
- 06:18–06:25 Platform, check board: want **S8 Richtung Wiesbaden**
  or **S9 Richtung Wiesbaden**. Position mid-platform (escalator
  exits at FRA are mid-platform too).
- 06:25–06:30 Board. Sit with bag on lap/rack, ticket screen open,
  set a 07:05 alarm labeled "FLUGHAFEN — aussteigen".

## 06:30–07:12 — S-Bahn ride (33–48 min, plan 42)

- 06:30–06:35 Departure. Text/check-in with family if needed; then
  phone to flight mode-ish (offline maps already downloaded).
- 06:35–07:00 Ride through Offenbach Ost → Frankfurt Hbf (stay
  seated — tourists exit here by mistake every day) → Frankfurt
  West/Griesheim → Flughafen. Landmarks: after Frankfurt Hbf the
  next airport-relevant stop announcement is "Frankfurt Flughafen".
- 07:05 Alarm rings → bag ready, stand, move to doors.
- 07:08–07:12 Alight **Frankfurt (M) Flughafen Regionalbahnhof**.
  Do NOT exit toward Fernbahnhof signs — follow **Terminal 1**
  (escalators straight up from platforms 1–3).

## 07:12–07:25 — Up into Terminal 1 (13 min)

- 07:12–07:18 Escalators to T1 arrivals/departures level. Orientation:
  halls A/B/C for Lufthansa; screens everywhere — find LH454, note
  check-in hall row + gate (expect a Z gate, e.g. Z66).
- 07:18–07:25 Walk to the assigned check-in hall/row. Toilets now
  (queues are short landside this early). Water fountain note: fill
  the bottle AFTER security, not here.

## 07:25–07:45 — Bag drop (20 min)

- 07:25–07:35 Queue (early = short). At counter: passport, booking
  code if asked; ESTA is verified electronically against the booking
  — if the agent flags documents, show the ESTA approval screenshot.
- 07:35–07:40 Bag tagged, boarding pass confirmed (gate + seat +
  group). Photograph the bag tag receipt.
- 07:40–07:45 Recheck gate on screens (Z gates can change).
  Decide: security now (recommended) — no more shopping landside.

## 07:45–08:15 — Security (30 min incl. queue)

- 07:45–07:55 Queue to trays. While queuing: laptop out, liquids
  bag (≤100 ml, 1 L bag) in hand, jacket/belt/watch off, pockets
  emptied into jacket, power bank in tray (never in checked bag).
- 07:55–08:05 Trays through, body scan, collect everything by the
  checklist: laptop, liquids, jacket, belt, watch, phone, wallet,
  power bank, boarding pass, passport. Count items: 9.
- 08:05–08:15 Fill water bottle at fountain. Walk toward
  **Passkontrolle / Z gates** signs. Do not follow B/C gates.

## 08:15–08:40 — Passport control + SkyLine to Z pier (25 min)

- 08:15–08:25 Automated gates (EasyPASS with e-passport) or staffed
  lanes; passport only, no questions outbound usually.
- 08:25–08:35 **SkyLine** people-mover to the Z pier (2 stops, ~5 min
  ride + walking). This is the step people underestimate — the Z pier
  is far.
- 08:35–08:40 Arrive Z pier. Confirm gate on the pier screens.
  Toilets, buy a sandwich/snack for the gate wait, refill water.

## 08:40–10:10 — Gate hold (90 min buffer, spend it well)

- 08:40–09:00 Eat properly (last real German food for 4 days),
  call/message home (free airport Wi-Fi), final wc.
- 09:00–09:30 Relax: podcast, the sights doc re-read, confirm hotel
  check-in time + Day-2 tour pickup in emails. Phone to full charge
  on a gate power outlet; power bank topped too.
- 09:30–09:40 Boarding usually starts (~40–50 min before dep).
  Be at the gate area, bag packed, boarding pass + passport in hand,
  group number known. Ignore the standing crowd until your group.
- 09:40–10:00 Board by groups. Extra US document check at the gate
  is normal — passport + boarding pass ready.
- 10:00–10:10 Seated: bag stowed, seatbelt, water + snacks in
  seat pocket, IFE on, watch set to California time (SFO = CEST−9).
  Continue: [09-the-flight.md](09-the-flight.md) for the 11.5 h plan.
