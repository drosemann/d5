# 25 — Campus Perimeter Walk

Public sidewalk only. Campus behind badges, sign and perimeter are the shrine. Companion to [04-sights](04-sights.md) and [19-per-meter](19-per-meter.md).

## The loop

- Start Like sign pullout, Bayfront Expy and Willow Rd. Not 1 Hacker Way gate, that is the wrong photo spot.
- Front, right flank, Sun Microsystems ghost logo on back, left flank, front again. Ten minutes minimum.
- East to Bayfront Park trailhead. West back along Willow to gate glimpse from sidewalk.
- Gate: badge readers, pale-blue bikes, shuttle buses. Photo from sidewalk, never block driveway. Security nods, nod back.

## What you are looking at

- Classic MPK ex-Sun, Building 16, plus MPK20 Frank Gehry mass: 430,000 sq ft, one giant room, ~2,800 people.
- Tunnel under Bayfront Expy links old and new. You cannot use it. Picture it anyway.
- MPK20 roof: 9-acre park, ~400 trees, half-mile loop. See [26-rooftop-loop](26-rooftop-loop.md).

## Rules

- Sidewalk only, stay off landscaping, drone rules strict near campus.
- Morning light best for front, sunset best for thumb silhouette. See [29-night-golden-hour](29-night-golden-hour.md).
- Water, sunscreen, hat. No shade at sign, full sun.
