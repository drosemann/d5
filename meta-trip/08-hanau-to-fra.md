# Hanau → FRA: Every Step

Assumes departure from Hanau city, Lufthansa long-haul from Terminal 1
(LH454 to SFO typically departs ~10:10 from a Z gate). Work backwards:
be at FRA T1 check-in **3 hours before departure** (~07:10).

## Step 0 — Night before (Hanau)

- Check in online (Lufthansa app), save boarding pass to phone +
  screenshot as backup
- ESTA approval, passport, return ticket: one pouch, photographed
- Pack to plan: fleece accessible (SFO evenings are cool, plane is cold)
- Charge phone + power bank to 100%; download offline Bay Area maps
- Set two alarms; check [RMV](https://www.rmv.de) / DB Navigator for
  S8/S9 disruptions on the Hanau–Frankfurt corridor

## Step 1 — Home → Hanau Hauptbahnhof

- Leave home ~**05:45** for a 10:10 flight (buffer for one missed S-Bahn)
- Tram/bus to Hanau Hbf, or ~€10 taxi if it's dark/early and buses
  are thin — factor this into the budget doc

## Step 2 — Ticket (~€7.80 single, RMV)

- Buy **Hanau → Frankfurt Flughafen Regionalbahnhof** single in the
  RMV app or at the machine (a plain Frankfurt-city ticket does NOT
  cover the airport leg — the airport has its own RMV fare level)
- Validate/stamp only if it's a paper ticket that requires it;
  phone tickets are ready to go
- Keep the ticket until you leave the airport station (controls happen)

## Step 3 — Hanau Hbf → Frankfurt Flughafen Regionalbahnhof

- Take **S8 (direction Wiesbaden) or S9 (direction Wiesbaden)** —
  both run Hanau → Offenbach → Frankfurt → Flughafen, no transfers
- Frequency ~every 15–30 min early morning; ride **33–48 min**
- Stay on past Frankfurt Hbf (the airport is ~12 min further west)
- Get off at **Frankfurt (M) Flughafen Regionalbahnhof** (NOT the
  Fernbahnhof — that's long-distance ICE; you want the regional one
  directly beneath **Terminal 1**)
- Follow the escalators up into Terminal 1 arrivals level

## Step 4 — Terminal 1 check-in (~07:10)

- Lufthansa long-haul: **Terminal 1, halls A/B/C**, USA departures
  leave from the **Z gates** (non-Schengen upper level, e.g. Z66)
- Bag drop: passport ready, ESTA is checked against your booking —
  if online check-in flagged documents, the agent clears it here
- Note your gate on the screens; Z gates mean passport control +
  a SkyLine train ride within the terminal — allow 20+ min to reach
  the gate from check-in

## Step 5 — Security + passport control

- Liquids bag (100 ml rule) and laptop out; belt/jacket off
- After security: follow signs to **Z** (passport control first,
  then the SkyLine people-mover to the Z pier)
- At the gate: boarding pass + passport again; US-bound flights get
  an extra document/ESTA verification at boarding

## Step 6 — Boarding (~09:30–10:00)

- Long-haul boarding starts ~40–50 min before departure, by groups
  (back rows first) — be at the gate when your group is called
- Window seat (left side) for Greenland views; aisle for leg freedom
- Continue in [09-the-flight.md](09-the-flight.md)
