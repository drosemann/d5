# On Video: Visit From the Couch First

Watch these before booking anything — they show what the tour buses
don't and what "no public access" really looks like.

## Meta HQ films

- **INSIDE Meta HQ in Menlo Park, California (FACEBOOK)**
  (~6.5 min) — the classic inside look at the campus.
  [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Sr-De6XBhBw)
- **Facebook HQ Tour** — walkthrough of the newer HQ spaces.
  [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jab3jGF6Npk)
- **Zuckerberg's MPK20 tour (2015)** — the viral short of the
  Gehry building's opening: one giant room, rooftop park, street-art
  walls. Search "Zuckerberg MPK20 tour" — widely re-uploaded.
- **Supercar Blondie: Inside Meta's HQ** — the influencer cut with
  the Disneyland-inspired zone.
  (search "supercarblondie meta headquarters" on YouTube)

## Context pieces (articles worth reading)

- Pocket-lint's **MPK20 opening gallery** — the #MPK20firstlook
  Instagram wave: custom art, the blue box, the roof.
  [Read](https://www.pocket-lint.com/apps/news/facebook/133408-take-a-peek-inside-facebook-s-new-hq-called-mpk20-and-designed-by-frank-gehry/)
- Richard Hartley's **"Step into Zuckworld"** — rollerblading,
  selfie stumps, and the Ikea-arrival critique of MPK20.
  [Read](http://www.richardhartley.com/2015/04/step-into-zuckworld-rollerblading-and-selfie-stumps-at-the-new-facebook-hq/)

## What the videos teach you

1. The inside is genuinely spectacular — and you won't see it;
   calibrate expectations to "sign + perimeter."
2. The Like sign is bigger than it looks on screen; the Sun-logo
   back is the detail everyone misses.
3. MPK20 reads as a blue warehouse from the road — the rooftop park
   is the hidden half.
