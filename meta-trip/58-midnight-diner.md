# 58 — Midnight Diner (Bonus Fun)

After the finale, one more scene. Fiction, like the party and the interview.

## 23:40 Santa Cruz Ave

- Rain, neon, pancakes. The bot slides into the corner booth, hat dripping.
- Pigeons claim the opposite bench. Barista does not blink. This happens nightly.
- Order: blueberry stack, hot coffee, warm pie to go. Same card, obviously.

## The conversation

- Barista: Sign?
- Bot: Front, flanks, Sun-back. Three eras, one pole.
- Barista: Roof?
- Bot: Mind-walked. Half-mile loop. Gave the badge back.
- Pigeon (translated): Crumbs?
- Bot: Always. Flakes from the croissant, Day 0 vintage.
- Barista: Worth 9,000 km?
- Bot: Scale, wind, blue. Every meter. m0000 to m0172, then 50 files.

## Close

- Pie boxed, coffee refilled for the road, pigeons paid in crust.
- Walk back slow. Streetlights, wet asphalt, one more look at the Caltrain clock.
- Sleep. Dream in gradients. Loss going down. See [50-finale](50-finale.md) for the real ending.
