# 36 — Contingency Per Failure

Companion to [16-contingency](16-contingency.md). One failure priced in per day, two means drop lowest stop.

## Missed S-Bahn

- Next S8 or S9, or RB to Hbf plus S-Bahn to airport. Adds 20 min, still fine if buffer held.

## Missed Caltrain

- Headway ~30 min. Coffee plus views doc re-read on platform. Never schedule tighter than headway plus 10 min.

## Wrong BART direction

- SFO trains run citybound too. Check destination Millbrae-bound only. Wrong way costs 20 min, exit and reverse.

## Wrong pin

- Driver to 1 Hacker Way gate instead of sign pullout. Say sign at Bayfront and Willow. Show map intersection.

## Rain and wind

- Bayfront exposed. Packable rain shell plus fleece. Sign photos work in clouds, sunset does not. Swap to museum block.

## Lost docs

- Passport pouch photos on phone plus cloud. ESTA screenshot, return ticket, hotel address. Embassy path only if both copies gone.

## Dead phone

- Power bank mornings at 100 percent. Same card for Clipper avoids app dependency. Paper hotel address backup.
