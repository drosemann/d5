# 31 — Picture Shelf

Still images beat video for planning angles. Use these before you go.

## Stock shelves

- [Facebook Like signs on Alamy](https://www.alamy.com/stock-photo/facebook-like.html): front, side, crowds, weather variants.
- [Menlo Park photos on Getty](https://www.gettyimages.in/photos/facebook-menlo-park): sign plus campus glass, palms, seasonal light.
- [Visitor video still on Alamy](https://www.alamy.com/video/menlo-park-california-usa-may-10-2016-a-visitor-makes-a-liking-facebook-sign-at-the-facebook-headquaters-in-menlo-park-california-582994060.html): scale reference with humans.

## Architecture shelves

- [Gehry HQ tour on Architizer](https://architizer.com/blog/practice/details/frank-gehrys-facebook-headquarters/): aerial plus interiors, 9-acre roof visible.
- [Workplace as Landscape award page](https://www.asla.org/awards-events-main-landing/honors-awards/pro-student-awards/2017-professional-awards/327767): planting, loop, respite pockets.
- [Greenroofs project page](https://www.greenroofs.com/projects/mpk20-facebook-meta-west-campus/): loop map and garden zones.

## How to use

- Save 6 angles offline: front, both flanks, Sun-back, gate, bayfront silhouette.
- Compare on site per [28-photo-spots](28-photo-spots.md). Note what felt different: scale, wind, blue.
