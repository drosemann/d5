# Logistics: Getting There and Around

## Flights (Germany → SFO)

- Destination airport: **San Francisco International (SFO)** —
  the only Bay Area airport with a direct rail link toward Menlo Park
- Typical routing from Germany: direct Frankfurt–SFO (Lufthansa/United,
  ~11 h) or 1-stop via London, Amsterdam, Paris, Madrid, Zurich
- Book 2–3 months out; shoulder season (Sep–Oct, Mar–Apr) is cheapest
  for both fares and hotels
- Typical return fare range: **€450–€900** economy depending on season

## Entry: ESTA (German citizens)

- No visa needed for tourism up to **90 days** (Visa Waiver Program)
- You need an approved **ESTA** before boarding:
  apply at **esta.cbp.dhs.gov** (official site — beware copycat fee sites)
- Fee ~**$40.62** (since Oct 2026), valid **2 years** or until passport
  expiry; apply **at least 72 h** before departure, ideally at booking time
- Carry: biometric passport (valid for the whole stay), ESTA approval
  printout/screenshot, return ticket, hotel address, proof of funds

## SFO → Menlo Park (no car)

1. **BART + Caltrain (recommended, ~$12, ~1 h):**
   SFO BART station → Millbrae, transfer to Caltrain southbound →
   Menlo Park station (every ~30 min, $5–7, 25 min ride)
2. **Rideshare/taxi:** ~30–45 min, **$50–70**, door to door —
   worth it with luggage or after a long flight
3. **SamTrans bus:** cheapest, slowest, every 15–20 min toward
   Palo Alto/Menlo Park corridors

## Getting around locally

- **Caltrain** is the spine: Menlo Park ↔ Palo Alto ↔ Mountain View ↔
  San Francisco ↔ San Jose; $3.50–8 per ride by distance
- Downtown Menlo Park, Stanford, and the Meta sign area are walkable
  or short bus/rideshare hops from Caltrain stations
- The Day-2 organized tour solves the campus-to-campus problem
  (Googleplex, Apple Park, and the museums are far apart by transit)
- Apple/Google maps work fine; download offline maps for the Bay Area
- Tipping: 15–20% in restaurants, $1–2 per bag, 10–15% for tour guides

## Money + phone

- Cards accepted everywhere (Visa/Mastercard); carry ~$100 cash backup
- eSIM or US prepaid (T-Mobile/AT&T tourist plans) — buy before
  departure or at SFO; free Wi-Fi in hotels, museums, Caltrain stations
