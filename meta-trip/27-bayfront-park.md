# 27 — Bedwell Bayfront Park

Next to the sign, free, windy, perfect jet-lag walk. 1600 Edgewood style marsh plus bay views.

## Why go

- Flat trails, ducks, salt ponds, Dumbarton Bridge. First sit-down of Day 0.
- Snack plus water plus sign photo message home. Greetings from the makers.
- Sunset option glows, jacket required. SFO wind rule applies even here.

## Micro route (45 min)

- Trailhead from Bayfront Expy pullout area. Cross at green, long wait normal.
- Main loop east, bay on left. Bench at first viewpoint.
- Turn at marsh overlook. Back same way, no maze.
- Rideshare back or M4 shuttle Willow stop weekday commute hours.

## Rules

- Stay on trails, marsh is protected. No swimming, water cold and muddy.
- Carry water out, no fountains inside. Sunscreen, no shade.
- Phone charged, maps pinned. Caltrain alarm logic still applies for return.
