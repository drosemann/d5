# 82 — Seasons Guide (Real Guide)

September or October stays the answer. Here is why, month by month.

## Sep-Oct (go)

- Warm days, clear bay, fog mostly mornings. Sign photos crisp, roof mind-walk pleasant.
- CHM open days easy, no holiday crowds. Flights mid-priced.

## May-Jun (good second)

- Long days, green hills lingering, June gloom mornings at the coast.
- Layers rule: fleece plus shell, SFO wind year-round.

## Nov-Feb (possible)

- Rain, short days, Bayfront mud. Museums shine, sign glows in storm light.
- Cheap flights, wet shoes. Packable shell plus spare socks.

## Jul-Aug (fog classic)

- Inland heat, coast fog, Mark Twain summer. Sign fine, bridge invisible.
- Book everything early, crowds peak.
