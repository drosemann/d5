# 47 — Souvenirs Checklist

Buy light, buy late, carry on only if possible.

## Meta orbit

- Notebook sticker from backpack counts. Never sticker the sign.
- Apple Park store exclusives only sold there. Google store smalls.
- Stanford bookstore postcard, CHM poster tube if you checked a bag.

## SF extension

- Ferry Building second pass, Chinatown tea or fans, cable car pin.
- Photos beat objects: 40 keepers culled from 200 beat 5 kg of stuff.

## Pack rule

- Souvenirs packed night before, liquids bagged, power bank in cabin never checked.
- Luggage hold at desk if late flight. Collect before Caltrain to SFO.
