# 38 — Hotels Compared

Base Menlo Park or Palo Alto nights 1 to 4. SF Union Square or Wharf nights 5 to 7 if extending.

## Menlo Park

- Downtown walkable to Caltrain and Santa Cruz Ave dinner. Best for sign mornings.
- Ask: early check-in for Day 0 drop, luggage hold Day 4, blackout curtains, quiet side.

## Palo Alto

- University Ave food scene, Stanford access. Slightly livelier nights, same Caltrain line.
- Tradeoff: 1 stop from Menlo Park, easy but adds transfer to sign mornings.

## SF extension

- 180 to 250 dollars night mid. Ferry Building grazing, cable cars, Golden Gate fog.
- BART to SFO ~30 min from downtown for late flights. See [17-extended](17-extended.md).

## Booking rules

- Free cancellation until week before. Confirm pickup point if tour operator serves Peninsula.
- Photo hotel address for CBP answer. Screenshot check-in and hold policy.
