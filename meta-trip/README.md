## The one-paragraph version

Meta HQ is **not open to the public** — no tours, no lobby, no entry.
The visit is a photo stop at the iconic Like sign, best combined with a
Silicon Valley driving tour (Googleplex, Apple Park Visitor Center,
Computer History Museum, Stanford, HP garage). Fly into **SFO**, take
**BART to Millbrae then Caltrain to Menlo Park** (~1 h, ~$12), stay in
Menlo Park or Palo Alto, and go in **September or October** for the best
weather. Budget roughly **€1,800–€2,800** for 4 days from Germany.
German citizens need an **ESTA** (~$40.62, apply ≥72 h before departure),
valid 2 years, 90 days max per stay.

## Key facts

- Address: 1 Hacker Way (Hacker Wy), Menlo Park, CA 94025, USA
- The Like sign sits at Bayfront Expressway & Willow Road; its back
  still shows the old **Sun Microsystems** logo — two tech eras, one sign
- Campus = historic MPK (ex-Sun, Building 16) + MPK20 (Frank Gehry,
  430,000 sq ft, 9-acre rooftop park, ~2,800 people in one giant room)
- Computer History Museum is **closed Mon–Tue** — plan around it
- Caltrain Millbrae → Menlo Park: every ~30 min, $5–7, 25 min
