# 4-Day Itinerary: Menlo Park + Silicon Valley

Assumes arrival at SFO, base in Menlo Park, no rental car (transit +
one organized tour day + one rideshare day). Museum hours verified
late 2026: Computer History Museum **closed Mon–Tue**, so start the
trip on a **Wednesday**.

## Day 1 (Wed) — Arrival + the Makers

- Land SFO, BART → Millbrae, Caltrain → Menlo Park (~1 h door to door)
- Check in, drop bags, lunch downtown Menlo Park
- Afternoon: walk/bus to **1 Hacker Way**
  - Like-sign photo from every angle, including the Sun-logo back
  - Stroll the publicly visible perimeter: MPK20's giant blue box,
    the tunnel/underpass arrival, Main Street frontage
  - Sunset at **Bedwell Bayfront Park** next door (bay views, trails)
- Dinner downtown; early night against jet lag

## Day 2 (Thu) — The Organized Tour Day

- Full-day small-group Silicon Valley tour from San Francisco
  (~9 h, typically: Meta photo stop, Googleplex, Computer History
  Museum, Stanford walking tour, Apple Park Visitor Center)
- Why a tour: no public transit between campuses, parking is painful,
  narration covers the Sun→Facebook lore you'd otherwise miss
- Evening: rest, sort photos

## Day 3 (Fri) — Deep Geek Day (self-guided)

- Morning: **Computer History Museum**, Mountain View
  (1401 N Shoreline Blvd; the *Revolution* exhibit alone is 19 galleries,
  ~1,100 objects — allow 3+ hours)
- Midday: **Google Visitor Experience**, Mountain View (public part
  of the Googleplex) + lunch
- Afternoon: **Stanford University** — 2-hour self-guided walk
  (Main Quad, Hoover Tower viewpoint, Rodin garden)
- Evening: Palo Alto dinner (University Ave)

## Day 4 (Sat) — Apple + Departure

- Morning: **Apple Park Visitor Center**, Cupertino
  (iPad VR tour of the spaceship campus, cafe, store)
- Detour: **HP garage** (the "birthplace of Silicon Valley",
  367 Addison Ave, Palo Alto — plaque + photo only)
- Afternoon: Caltrain → Millbrae → BART → SFO, fly home

## If you have a 5th day

- San Francisco proper: Ferry Building, Golden Gate, Musee Mecanique
- Or: Intel Museum (Santa Clara, closed weekends — weekday only),
  NASA Ames visitor corner, Santa Cruz beach + boardwalk
