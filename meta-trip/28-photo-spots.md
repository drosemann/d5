# 28 — Photo Spots (Every Angle)

The sign is the shoot. Campus is background only, from public land.

## The six keepers

- Wide front: sign plus palms plus glass. Crouch low, thumb against sky.
- Portrait: you plus thumb. Ask a fellow pilgrim, swap and tap for them.
- Right flank macro: paint, bolts, blue sky reflections.
- Sun-back ghost logo: the old Sun Microsystems mark. Do not miss it.
- Gate from sidewalk: badge readers and bikes, never block driveway.
- Bayfront silhouette: thumb black against orange at sunset.

## Settings

- Morning: front lit, fewer crowds. Midday: harsh, use shade side detail. Sunset: silhouette and glow per [29-night-golden-hour](29-night-golden-hour.md).
- Video pan 10 seconds, no talking, just wind. Time-lapse 30 seconds of tourists orbiting.
- Backup to cloud on Caltrain Wi-Fi. Cull 200 to 40 keepers same night.

## Etiquette

- Never climb sign, never stick stickers on it. Notebook sticker only.
- Tripod off walkway. Share the front spot, 2 min each at busy times.
