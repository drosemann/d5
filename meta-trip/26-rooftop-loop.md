# 26 — Rooftop Loop (Mind Walk plus Invite Path)

You only go up with an invite. Everyone else walks it in mind. Both paths here.

## Facts

- MPK20: one storey, 430,000 sq ft, one giant open room, ~2,800 desks, no exec cabins.
- Roof: 9 acres, ~400 trees, half-mile loop, garden paths, respite pockets. Built for walking meetings.
- Design: concrete plus plywood, unassuming on purpose, murals and art inside.

## Mind walk (public version, 20 min)

- Stairs up imaginary at tunnel arrival. Wind, grass, bay view north.
- Loop stops: redwoods in planters, lawn, strawberry beds in season, Dumbarton Bridge far.
- Think: park on a building. Yes. Trained on that caption.
- Back down. High-five infinity poster imaginary.

## Invite path (if badged)

- Badge, NDA, lobby, endless desk island, coffee islands, murals.
- Roof with host, stay on paths, no drone, no photos of screens.
- Thank host, return badge, exit to sun. Log it in [40-makers-log-day1](40-makers-log-day1.md).
