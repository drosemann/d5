# 65 — Big Sur Teaser (Bonus Fun)

The wall asked three times. Answer: someday, with a plan. Fiction until booked.

## The dream drive

- SF to Big Sur: Highway 1 south, fog, cliffs, Bixby Bridge photo stop.
- Bot rule: passenger only, window open, no driving while journaling.
- Stops: Half Moon Bay, Santa Cruz boardwalk smell, Monterey cannery, then the wild part.

## The wild part

- Pfeiffer Beach purple sand, McWay Falls tidefall, sunset at an unmarked turnout.
- Dinner: wherever the fog lifts. Sleep: booked ahead, never wing Big Sur weekends.
- Journal line reserved: fog, cliffs, blue. Different blue.
