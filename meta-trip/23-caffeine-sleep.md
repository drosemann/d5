# 23 — Caffeine and Sleep Strategy

The whole trip is a jet lag mission. Caffeine half-life ~6 h. Use it like medicine.

## Outbound

- Last Hanau coffee 05:30. Gate coffee 08:40 allowed, small.
- Flight coffee only with mid-flight snack round, around T+165. Never after T+180.
- Sunglasses trick: shade open while cabin bright, brain takes light cues.
- Sleep attempt T+255 to T+330 with mask and plugs. Aim 2 to 3 h, one full cycle.
- Landing coffee only after breakfast service. It is morning in SFO, correct timezone.

## Day 0 Menlo Park

- No nap. Rule from arrival file: no sitting still longer than train rides until 21:00.
- Dinner water, no alcohol night one. Alcohol multiplies jet lag.
- Lights out 21:00 local. Earplugs, blackout, cool room.
- Wake 02:00 trick: water sip, no phone, back to sleep.

## Days 1 to 4

- One Philz experience, hotel coffee otherwise. Airport coffee 6 dollars plus, fill before security.
- No caffeine after 14:00 local days 1 to 3. Protects 21:00 sleep.
- Morning light walk daily: Bayfront, Stanford Quad, Embarcadero. Light beats coffee.

## Checklist

- [ ] Caffeine log: time and dose
- [ ] Mask and plugs in daypack every morning
- [ ] 21:00 lights out day 0, 22:00 after
