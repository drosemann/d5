# The Flight: LH454 FRA → SFO, Hour by Hour

Reference flight: **Lufthansa LH454**, Frankfurt T1 (Z gates, e.g. Z66)
→ San Francisco International Terminal (e.g. gate G13). Scheduled
**~10:10 → ~12:41 local**, i.e. **~11.5 hours** in the air. Return is
LH455, SFO ~15:00 → FRA. Times shift by season — confirm yours.

## Before takeoff (10:10)

- Pushback, safety demo, climb-out over the Taunus → set watch to
  California time now (CET−9 h PST / −8 h PDT): when you land it will
  feel like ~21:40 Hanau time. The whole flight is a jet-lag mission.
- Recline planning: sleep the second half, stay awake the first half

## Hours 0–2 — Settle in (~10:10–12:10 CET)

- First drink service + lunch (~1 h after takeoff)
- Eat on California schedule if you can (it's ~1–3 AM body-clock…
  realistically just eat, you'll need it)
- Walk the cabin once; water bottle filled — drink constantly
- Entertainment setup: download nothing, IFE has films/series/games;
  window seat gets Greenland later

## Hours 2–6 — Stay awake (~12:10–16:10 CET)

- Films, the moving map, the d5 codebase in your head — stay up
- Coffee with the mid-flight snack round, not later (caffeine has a
  6 h half-life and you must sleep in hour 7+)
- Sunglasses trick: keep the shade open while the cabin is bright,
  your brain takes light cues for the new timezone
- Walk + stretch every ~2 h (DVT prevention, real, do it)

## Hours 6–9 — Sleep shift (~16:10–19:10 CET)

- Eye mask, earplugs/noise-cancelling, blanket, recline
- Aim for 2–3 h of actual sleep — that is a full cycle and the
  difference between a usable Day 1 and a zombie Day 1
- Skip the second meal if you're sleeping through it; the crew wakes
  you for descent prep anyway

## Hours 9–11.5 — Descent (~19:10–21:40 CET / ~10:10–12:41 SFO)

- Breakfast service ~90 min before landing
- Fill in nothing (no paper customs form for ESTA travelers —
  it's all electronic + the CBP officer interview)
- Charged phone + power bank on (you'll need maps immediately),
  boarding pass kept (occasionally re-checked at connections — n/a here)
- Landing over the bay, taxi to the **International Terminal**

## Landing: SFO International Terminal, step by step

1. Follow **Arrivals / Baggage Claim**, down to the CBP hall
2. **Immigration (CBP):** join the **ESTA/Visa Waiver** line
   (NOT the US-citizens line, NOT crew). Have ready: passport open
   at photo page, "tourism, 4 days, Menlo Park hotel" as your answer,
   return ticket on phone. Photo + fingerprints taken. Typical wait
   15–60 min depending on how many wide-bodies landed with you.
3. **Baggage claim:** find your carousel on the screens (belt info
   like "CL1" on the arrival boards), collect bag
4. **Customs:** walk through the nothing-to-declare exit (spot checks
   are rare; food/plant products are the classic trap — declare them)
5. You are landside. It is ~13:00 California time, ~22:00 Hanau time.
   Stay awake until at least 21:00 local — this is the whole game.
6. Continue in [10-sfo-to-hq.md](10-sfo-to-hq.md) — regroup, transit
   tickets, AirTrain, BART.
