# Stay + Food: Menlo Park and Palo Alto

## Where to stay

Base yourself in **Menlo Park** (walkable to Caltrain, closest to HQ)
or **Palo Alto** (livelier evenings, Stanford next door). Indicative
2026 prices — verify at booking time:

| Hotel | Vibe | Indicative from |
|---|---|---|
| citizenM Menlo Park | Design-budget, techy crowd | ~$135–190 |
| Hotel Lucent | Mid-range, quiet | ~mid $100s |
| Park James | Boutique 4★ | ~$210–320 |
| Hotel Nia, Autograph Collection | Upscale 5★ | ~$220–290 |
| Rosewood Sand Hill | Splurge, VC-deal country | ~$1,500 |
| Menlo Park Inn / Stanford Park Hotel | Solid classics | ~$150–250 |

Cheaper play: private rooms near Meta/tech hubs on momondo/Airbnb
(from ~$90), or stay in San Francisco and day-trip down by Caltrain
(cheaper hotels, +1 h commute each way).

## Where to eat

- **Downtown Menlo Park** (Santa Cruz Ave): cafes, ramen, tacos —
  easy walking dinner after Day 1
- **University Ave, Palo Alto**: the classic founder-dinner street;
  good for the Day 3 evening
- **Apple Park Visitor Center cafe** (Day 4): coffee with a spaceship view
- **Stanford campus eateries** (Day 3 lunch option)
- Bay Area staples to try: In-N-Out Burger, Philz Coffee, a Mission
  burrito if you pass through SF, clam chowder in a bread bowl
- Grocery runs: Trader Joe's and Whole Foods cover picnic supplies
  for Bedwell Bayfront Park sunset

## Practical notes

- Hotel taxes add ~14% on top of Bay Area room rates — budget for it
- Check-in is usually 3–4 PM; Day 1 lands around midday, so pick a
  hotel with luggage hold or a late checkout option for Day 4
- Tap water is safe; Bay Area September/October is warm days, cool
  evenings — one fleece covers it (see checklist)
