# 22 — Food Hour by Hour

Hourly companion to [14-food](14-food.md). Jet lag kills decisions, so eat by clock.

## Flight clock CET

- 05:30 Home: light yogurt and coffee only. Heavy food plus 11.5 h cabin equals regret.
- 08:40 FRA gate: proper second breakfast airside. Sandwiches over croissants. Budget 10 to 15 euro.
- 11:10 CET lunch aloft: eat everything, best meal of flight. Water, no second coffee yet.
- 14:00 CET mid-flight snack: take it even if not hungry.
- 19:00 CET breakfast before landing: eat, teeth, then nothing until Menlo Park. Coffee allowed, it is morning in SFO.

## Menlo Park clock local

- 15:00 Sign photos first, snack second. Blood sugar decides your evening.
- 18:30 Dinner Santa Cruz Ave: primary [yeobo darling](https://www.colette.com) style Korean comfort, backup Camper, tired option pizza slice. No alcohol night one.
- Day 1 breakfast: hotel or Mademoiselle Colette to go, ~10 dollars.
- Day 1 lunch: tour or museum cafe, 15 to 20 dollars.
- Day 1 dinner: Eylan or Bistro Vida, 40 to 60 dollars. Celebration dinner, sign is done.
- Day 2 breakfast: Cafe Borrone patio. Lunch: museum cafe or Castro Street. Dinner: University Ave Palo Alto.
- Day 3 breakfast: Philz Mint Mojito ritual. Lunch: Apple Park Visitor Center cafe. SFO dinner: Intl Terminal G options.

## Grocery and water rules

- Trader Joe run day 0 or 1: snacks, fruit, water for Caltrain and Bayfront picnic, ~20 dollars covers trip.
- Tap water safe everywhere. One bottle refilled all trip. Dehydration magnifies jet lag more than food.
- Total mid budget ~300 euro: one 50 dollar dinner, 25 dollar lunches, 10 to 15 dollar breakfasts.
