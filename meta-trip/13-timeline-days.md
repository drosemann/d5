# Tight Timelines: Day 2, Day 3, Day 4 + Return

Day 1 is covered minute-by-minute in [12-timeline-arrival.md](12-timeline-arrival.md).
These run in 15–30 min blocks — tight enough to follow, loose enough
to survive reality. Every transfer keeps a named buffer.

## Day 2 (Thu) — Organized tour day (~09:00–18:00)

Typical small-group/full-day Silicon Valley tour from SF (verify your
operator's exact pickup when booking):

- 08:00–08:30 Breakfast, daypack (water, power bank, layers, snacks),
  confirm pickup point/time in email/app.
- 08:30–09:00 Pickup (usually SF Union Square area or your hotel if
  the operator serves the Peninsula — confirm at booking).
- 09:00–10:00 Drive to first stop (narration: Valley history en route).
- 10:00–10:20 **Meta HQ photo stop** (same sign as Day 1 — this time
  with the tour narration: Sun campus history, MPK20 stats).
- 10:20–11:00 Drive to **Googleplex** (photo stop, Visitor Experience
  if scheduled).
- 11:00–13:30 **Computer History Museum** (guided or free window —
  note what you skip for Day 3's deep visit).
- 13:30–14:15 Lunch (tour-included or own — confirm beforehand).
- 14:15–16:15 **Stanford** walking tour (~2 h: Quad, Church, Hoover).
- 16:15–17:00 Drive to **Apple Park Visitor Center** (VR + store).
- 17:00–18:00 Return drive. Dinner, photo backup (cloud + laptop),
  charge everything. Buffer: tour traffic on US-101 can add 30 min.

## Day 3 (Fri) — Self-guided deep day (~09:00–20:00)

- 08:30–09:00 Breakfast, Caltrain check (southbound to Mountain View,
  ~20 min from Menlo Park).
- 09:00–09:30 Caltrain Menlo Park → Mountain View. Walk/bus to
  1401 N Shoreline Blvd.
- 09:30–12:30 **Computer History Museum** (3 h: *Revolution* in full,
  demo days/tours if offered — check the desk on entry).
- 12:30–13:30 Lunch (museum cafe or Mountain View Castro Street).
- 13:30–14:30 **Google Visitor Experience** (public Googleplex corner,
  store, photo with the Android statues if present).
- 14:30–15:00 Caltrain Mountain View → Palo Alto.
- 15:00–17:30 **Stanford** self-guided loop (Quad → Church → Hoover
  Tower viewpoint → Rodin garden; the Dish if legs allow, +1 h).
- 17:30–18:00 Caltrain Palo Alto → Menlo Park.
- 18:00–20:00 Dinner on University Ave, Palo Alto (founder-street
  experience), early night.

## Day 4 (Sat) — Apple + departure (~09:00–evening)

- 08:30–09:00 Breakfast, bags packed, checkout (luggage hold at desk).
- 09:00–09:45 Caltrain Menlo Park → Sunnyvale/Mountain View +
  rideshare, or direct rideshare to **Apple Park Visitor Center**
  (Cupertino — no direct rail; budget ~30–45 min, ~$25–40).
- 09:45–11:45 **Apple Park VC** (iPad VR tour, exhibition, terrace
  ring view, cafe, store exclusives).
- 11:45–12:30 Rideshare to Palo Alto, **HP garage** (367 Addison Ave:
  plaque photo, 10 min) + lunch.
- 12:30–13:00 Collect luggage at hotel.
- 13:00–14:00 Caltrain Menlo Park → Millbrae → BART → SFO (reverse
  of arrival; LH455 ~15:00 dep Intl Terminal — at SFO by ~13:30
  for a 15:00 flight is tight; leave Menlo Park by **12:00**).
- 14:00–15:00 SFO check-in, security (Intl Terminal, smaller queues
  than FRA usually), gate.
- 15:00 Wheels up SFO (LH455). Land FRA next morning ~10:00–11:00
  local (+1 day). S8/S9 back to Hanau Hbf (reverse of outbound,
  ~45 min, same €7.80 ticket), home by ~13:00. Trip complete.

## Buffer rules (all days)

- Never schedule transfers tighter than the published headway + 10 min
  (Caltrain 30 min headway → plan 40).
- One missed connection per day is priced in; two means dropping the
  lowest-priority stop (order: HP garage first, Google second).
- Phone battery is a single point of failure: power bank leaves the
  hotel every morning at 100%.
