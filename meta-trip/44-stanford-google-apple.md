# 44 — Stanford, Google, Apple Deep

The three stops that orbit the sign. Each half day minimum if honest.

## Stanford

- Quad plus Church plus Hoover Tower viewpoint plus Rodin garden. 2 h walking, shoes complain.
- Dish trail plus 1 h only if legs allow. Free, hilly, views.
- Food: Stanford area or museum cafe if combined with CHM day.

## Googleplex

- Photo stop plus Visitor Experience public corner, store, Android statues if present.
- Confirm tour inclusion at booking: included versus own lunch changes timing.
- Pair with CHM same day, both Mountain View. See [37-transit-timetables](37-transit-timetables.md).

## Apple Park Visitor Center

- Cupertino, no direct rail. Budget 30 to 45 min plus 25 to 40 dollars rideshare.
- iPad VR, exhibition, terrace ring view, cafe, store exclusives. 2 h.
- Pair with HP garage plaque same morning, then luggage and SFO.

## Order rule

- Tour day does all three fast with narration. Deep days split: CHM plus Google one day, Stanford plus Apple another.
