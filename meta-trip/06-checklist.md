# Checklist: Before You Go, Pack, On Site

## 4+ weeks before

- [ ] Passport biometric + valid through the whole trip
- [ ] ESTA at esta.cbp.dhs.gov (~$40.62, ≥72 h before departure)
- [ ] Flights Germany → SFO booked (shoulder season = cheaper)
- [ ] Hotel in Menlo Park or Palo Alto booked (check 3–4 PM check-in)
- [ ] Day-2 Silicon Valley tour booked (confirm Meta/Google/CHM/
      Stanford/Apple stops in the itinerary)
- [ ] Travel insurance with US health coverage (non-negotiable —
      US healthcare bills are brutal)
- [ ] eSIM/US prepaid plan sorted

## Week before

- [ ] Caltrain + BART apps installed, offline Bay Area maps downloaded
- [ ] Computer History Museum hours re-checked (closed Mon–Tue)
- [ ] Return Caltrain/Millbrae→SFO timing planned for departure day
- [ ] Camera/phone storage cleared, power bank packed
- [ ] Notify bank of US travel (avoid card blocks)

## Pack

- [ ] Layers: warm days, cool Bay evenings — one fleece + windbreaker
- [ ] Comfortable walking shoes (Stanford loop, Bayfront trails)
- [ ] Sunglasses + sunscreen (that California sun)
- [ ] Power bank, US plug adapter (Type A/B)
- [ ] Snacks for Caltrain rides, reusable water bottle
- [ ] Small backpack for museum days

## On site

- [ ] Like-sign photo: front, both sides, and the Sun-logo back
- [ ] Bedwell Bayfront sunset, Day 1
- [ ] CHM *Revolution* exhibit fully walked, Day 3
- [ ] Stanford Hoover Tower viewpoint, Day 3
- [ ] Apple Park VR tour + terrace, Day 4
- [ ] HP garage plaque, Day 4
- [ ] Receipts kept for the budget post-mortem
