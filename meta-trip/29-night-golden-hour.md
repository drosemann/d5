# 29 — Golden Hour and Night

Second visit is worth it. Fewer crowds, better light, colder wind.

## Sunset run

- Rideshare Menlo Park to sign ~10 min. Arrive 30 min before sunset.
- Front glows, then thumb goes black against orange. Shoot both phases.
- Jacket on. Bayfront wind cuts through fleece. Hat stays on.

## Blue hour

- 15 min after sunset: sky purple, sign base lights on, traffic trails on Bayfront Expy.
- Tripod or curb brace. Same 6 shots as [28-photo-spots](28-photo-spots.md), new light.
- Do not walk Bayfront Park trails in dark. Perimeter and sign only.

## Return

- Rideshare back before full dark. Downtown dinner, hot chocolate, feet up.
- Charge everything. Photo backup same ritual. Tomorrow is museum day.
