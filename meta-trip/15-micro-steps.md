# 1-Minute Micro-Steps: The Three Mazes

The three places where minutes actually get lost. Each step is one
action, roughly one minute. Print this page or keep it open on the phone.

## Maze 1 — FRA T1 check-in → Z gate (~25 min, 25 steps)

1. Bag tag in hand, follow **Z** signs (never B/C).
2. Escalators/stairs down toward security lanes.
3. Liquids bag out of carry-on, into hand.
4. Laptop out, into hand.
5. Jacket/belt/watch off, into tray later — hold them.
6. Pockets emptied into jacket pockets.
7. Join shortest open lane (families = slow, business = fast).
8. Tray 1: jacket + watch + belt + phone + wallet.
9. Tray 2: laptop alone, nothing on top.
10. Tray 3: bag + liquids bag on top + shoes if required.
11. Walk through scanner, arms up, wait for green.
12. Collect trays in order; 9-item count: laptop, liquids, jacket,
    belt, watch, phone, wallet, power bank, boarding pass.
13. Shoes/gear back on at the bench, not in the lane.
14. Water fountain: fill bottle now.
15. Follow **Passkontrolle / Z** signs.
16. e-passport gate: passport photo page down, look at camera.
17. Through: keep boarding pass ready, second check possible.
18. Follow **SkyLine** signs (people-mover to Z pier).
19. Board SkyLine, 2 stops, stay on to Z.
20. Exit SkyLine, read pier screens: confirm Z gate number.
21. Walk the pier (long, moving walkways — stand right).
22. Gate found: check screen again (Z gates change).
23. Toilets now (queues at boarding are worse).
24. Buy gate snack + refill water.
25. Sit near the gate desk, charger in, boarding pass + passport out.

## Maze 2 — SFO Intl → Caltrain seat (~35 min, 20 steps)

1. Landside: bathroom + water (first 3 min, no dawdling).
2. SIM/data check: US signal or airport Wi-Fi.
3. Pin Menlo Park Caltrain + hotel in maps.
4. Decision: transit vs rideshare (only switch if wrecked).
5. Follow **AirTrain** signs (Red or Blue).
6. Board AirTrain.
7. Alight **Garage G / BART Station**.
8. BART hall: machines or Info Desk (by AirTrain escalator).
9. Buy Clipper ($3) + load ~$20, or pick contactless card.
10. Tap in (same card you'll tap out with — always).
11. Read destination sign: board **Millbrae-bound** only.
12. Ride 2 stops (~10 min). Bag on lap, watch for Millbrae.
13. Alight Millbrae (terminus this way).
14. Follow **Caltrain** transfer signs (same complex).
15. Tap Clipper at Caltrain reader (~$5–7) or app ticket.
16. Read board: any **southbound** stopping at Menlo Park.
17. Platform position: middle cars, doors open widest.
18. Board, sit, set alarm "MENLO PARK — aussteigen".
19. After Palo Alto: stand, bag ready, move to doors.
20. Alight Menlo Park. Breathe Bay Area air. Done.

## Maze 3 — Menlo Park station → the sign (~15 min by rideshare)

1. Exit station to the pickup curb (downtown side).
2. Open rideshare app (coverage is fine here).
3. Destination: **"Meta Like sign, Bayfront Expressway & Willow Rd"**
   — not "1 Hacker Way" (that's the gate, wrong photo spot).
4. Confirm pin shows the intersection, not the campus interior.
5. Ride ~8–10 min east across US-101 toward the bay.
6. Driver: "please stop at the sign pullout, not the gate."
7. Out. Verify: giant thumbs-up in front of you.
8. Walk the loop: front → sides → **Sun-logo back**.
9. Photos done → perimeter walk or Bayfront Park (adjacent).
10. Rideshare back or M4 shuttle from Willow Rd stop (weekday
    commute hours) / SamTrans 281.
