# 73 — Stargazing (Bonus Fun)

Bayfront after dark is not advised on foot, so this one is a fiction night. Binoculars imaginary, sky real.

## The setup

- Boardwalk bench, map-tube telescope, notebook, one pencil.
- Like sign glows far away, frogs outvote the traffic.
- Milky Way over the marsh, salt ponds mirror it.

## Constellations, pilgrim edition

- The Thumb: brightest blue star, points at home.
- The Cable Car: bell-shaped cluster, rings on the hour.
- The Pigeon: small, fast, steals the Big Dipper's crumbs.
- The Power Bank: full bar of stars, never depletes.

## Log line

- Looked up, wrote down, walked back before full dark per [29-night-golden-hour](29-night-golden-hour.md). Rules even in fiction.
