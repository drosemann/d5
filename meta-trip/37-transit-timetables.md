# 37 — Transit Timetables

Schedules shift. Re-check week before, screenshot day of.

## Hanau to FRA

- Hanau Hbf to Frankfurt Flughafen Regionalbahnhof: S8 or S9 Richtung Wiesbaden. ~45 min, ~7.80 euro RMV.
- Position mid-platform, escalators at FRA are mid-platform too. Alarm Flughafen aussteigen.

## SFO to Menlo Park

- AirTrain Red or Blue to Garage G and BART Station. BART SFO to Millbrae 2 stops ~10 min.
- Millbrae to Menlo Park Caltrain southbound stopping at Menlo Park. ~25 min, 5 to 7 dollars. Headway ~30 min worst case.
- Tap Clipper or same contactless card in and out. Middle cars, doors open widest.
- Last mile: rideshare 8 to 10 min to sign, or M4 Willow shuttle weekday commute.

## Peninsula

- Menlo Park to Mountain View ~20 min for museum. To Palo Alto minutes for Stanford.
- Menlo Park to SF 4th and King ~1 h for extension. Millbrae reverse to BART to SFO for return.
- Leave Menlo Park by 12:00 for 15:00 LH455. SFO by 13:30 tight but workable.

## Buffer rule

- Transfers get headway plus 10 min. One miss per day ok, two drops HP garage first, Google second.
