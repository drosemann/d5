# 24 — Packing Per Item

Every item earns its grams. Cabin target under 8 kg if no checked bag.

## Pouch (never leaves body)

- Passport, ESTA screenshot printed plus phone, return ticket, hotel addresses, insurance card.
- Phone, power bank 100 percent, cables, US plug adapter.
- Bank card for Clipper contactless plus backup card. Same card tap in and out always.

## Cabin comfort

- Eye mask, earplugs plus noise cancelling, neck pillow if you use one, fleece on top of bag.
- Water bottle empty through security, fill after. Snacks: nuts and bar.
- Pen, tissues, lip balm, toothbrush mini. Cabin air dries everything.

## Daypack Menlo Park

- Water, power bank at 100 percent every morning, layers for SFO wind, sunscreen.
- Maps offline: Menlo Park Caltrain, Like sign pin, hotel. Screenshots of southbound departures.
- Swimsuit? No. Bay is cold and muddy. Walking shoes broken in, not new.

## Do not pack

- Second laptop, big camera tripod, full towel, paper guidebooks. Phone plus one cable strip covers it.
- Food and plant products for US entry. Classic customs trap.

## Lay-out ritual

- Night before: travel outfit plus fleece accessible, chargers on strip, daypack packed, alarms set: 05:30 home, 07:05 Flughafen, Menlo Park stop later.
- Photo bag and pouch. Weigh mentally. Doors locked, go.
