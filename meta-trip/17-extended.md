# 7-Day Extended Itinerary: Valley + San Francisco

The 4-day plan ([01-itinerary.md](01-itinerary.md)) with three San
Francisco days grafted on — for when the flight is the expensive
part and the days are cheap. Base shifts: nights 1–4 Menlo Park,
nights 5–7 San Francisco (Union Square / Fisherman's Wharf area).

## Days 0–3 — unchanged (Menlo Park base)

Follow [01-itinerary.md](01-itinerary.md) and the minute timelines
exactly. The only change: pack an overnight-ready split on Day 3
evening (SF bag vs laundry bag) to keep Day 4 fast.

## Day 4 (Sun) — Menlo Park → San Francisco

- Morning: lie-in, brunch on Santa Cruz Ave (recovery morning —
  the 4-day sprint earned it).
- Midday: Caltrain Menlo Park → SF 4th & King (~1 h), check into
  SF hotel, drop bags.
- Afternoon: Ferry Building (food hall grazing lunch) + Embarcadero
  waterfront walk to Fisherman's Wharf.
- Evening: Fisherman's Wharf classics — clam chowder bread bowl,
  sea lions at Pier 39, Musée Mécanique (free antique arcade,
  genuinely wonderful). Sunset over the bay.

## Day 5 (Mon) — Icons day

- Morning: Powell/Mason cable car → Lombard Street (crookedest block
  photo) → walk down to the Wharf.
- Midday: Alcatraz? Only with tickets booked months ahead — if none,
  substitute the USS Pampanito + Maritime Museum (no booking pain).
- Afternoon: Golden Gate — bus/bike to the bridge, walk the first
  span (windbreaker mandatory, fog probable, glorious anyway).
- Evening: Mission District dinner (burrito mission: La Taqueria or
  El Farolito) + Dolores Park sunset if legs allow.

## Day 6 (Tue) — Culture + departure prep

- Morning: Golden Gate Park — California Academy of Sciences OR
  de Young Museum (pick one, 3 h) + park stroll.
- Midday: Haight-Ashbury walk (5 min from the park) + lunch.
- Afternoon: souvenirs (Ferry Building second pass / Chinatown),
  pack, lay out travel clothes, charge everything, hotel luggage
  hold if the flight is late.
- If LH455 departs ~15:00 like the 4-day plan, this day compresses:
  morning museum only, BART to SFO by ~12:00.

## What the 3 extra days cost (per person, mid level)

- SF hotel 3n: ~$180–250/night → ~€500–700
- Food 3d @ ~$75/d: ~€210
- Transit (Muni day passes, cable car ~$8/ride): ~€40
- Alcatraz (if booked): ~$45 · Museums: ~$25–40
- **Extension total: ~€800–1,000** → 7-day trip ≈ **€2,600–3,200**

## When NOT to extend

- First US trip + tight budget: the 4-day plan is the complete
  pilgrimage; SF deserves its own trip later.
- CHM-closed risk doesn't apply (SF sights are daily), but Alcatraz
  without advance tickets is a guaranteed disappointment — book it
  the day you book flights, or strike it now.
