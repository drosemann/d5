# 39 — Restaurants Compared

Named places with backups. Jet lag kills decisions, pick by smell and queue.

## Menlo Park Santa Cruz Ave

- yeobo darling: Korean-Taiwanese comfort, Michelin-star couple, Top-100. Best tired-night food.
- Camper: California sunlit corner, pork ragu rigatoni. Celebration backup.
- Mademoiselle Colette: French bakery to go. Philz Mint Mojito ritual separate.

## Palo Alto University Ave

- Eylan Indian Bib Gourmand or Bistro Vida full bar for Day 1 celebration.
- Local Union 271 pizza patio or deli classic for cheap night.

## Mountain View Castro Street

- Lunch near museum. Longest queue equals best. 15 to 20 dollars, fast.

## Cupertino and SFO

- Apple Park Visitor Center cafe with spaceship view. SFO Intl G: 1300 on Fillmore sit-down, Mustards, fast Gotts or Bun Mee.

## Rules

- Water with dinner, no alcohol night one. Trader Joe snacks cover Caltrain gaps. See [22-food-hourly](22-food-hourly.md).
