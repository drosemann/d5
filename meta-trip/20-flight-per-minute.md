# 20 — Flight Per 15 Minutes: LH454 FRA to SFO

Companion to [09-the-flight.md](09-the-flight.md). Same flight: Lufthansa LH454, T1 Z to SFO Intl, ~10:10 to ~12:41 local, ~11.5 h air.
Rule from the base file holds: sleep second half, stay awake first half, drink constantly, walk every 2 h.

## T+00 to T+02h — Climb and lunch

- T+00 Pushback 10:10. Safety demo. Phones to flight mode.
- T+15 Climb over Taunus. Set watch to California time now. It will feel like 21:40 Hanau at landing.
- T+30 Seatbelt sign off. Water bottle out. First sip.
- T+45 First drink round. Tomato juice or water. No alcohol yet.
- T+60 Lunch trays. Eat everything. This is the best meal of the flight per [14-food](14-food.md).
- T+75 Tray cleared. Teeth? No, too early. Walk cabin once, stretch calves.
- T+90 IFE setup. Pick a long film. Window shade open, light cues for new timezone.
- T+105 Second water. Bathroom queue short now, go now.
- T+120 Greenland is still hours away. Map check. Blood sugar good.

## T+02h to T+06h — Stay awake block

- T+135 Film part 2. Sit upright. No recline yet.
- T+150 Mid-flight snack round appears. Take it even if not hungry. Blood sugar on landing decides your evening.
- T+165 Coffee now, not later. Caffeine half-life is ~6 h and you must sleep in hour 7+.
- T+180 Stand, walk to galley and back. Ankle circles. Real DVT prevention, do it.
- T+195 Water again. Bottle should be half empty by now or you are behind.
- T+210 Second film or codebase in head. Stay up.
- T+225 Sunglasses trick: keep shade open while cabin bright.
- T+240 Six hours in. Bathroom, face wash, fresh shirt if you packed one.

## T+06h to T+09h — Sleep shift

- T+255 Recline. Eye mask, earplugs, blanket. Phone to Do Not Disturb.
- T+270 Aim for sleep now. 2 to 3 h real sleep is one full cycle and the difference between usable Day 1 and zombie Day 1.
- T+285 If awake, do not check time every 5 min. Breathing, podcast low.
- T+300 Crew dim lights. Good sign. Stay reclined.
- T+315 Second meal passes. Skip it if sleeping. Crew wakes you for descent anyway.
- T+330 Wake naturally or by crew nudge. Sit up slowly. Water immediately.
- T+345 Face wash. Glasses on. Brain reboot.

## T+09h to T+11.5h — Breakfast and descent

- T+360 Breakfast service ~90 min before landing. Eat, then teeth, then no more food until Menlo Park.
- T+375 Coffee only now if you must. It is ~10:00 SFO time, correct timezone for caffeine.
- T+390 Phone and power bank on and charging. You need maps immediately after landing.
- T+405 No paper customs form for ESTA travelers. All electronic plus CBP interview.
- T+420 Landing over the bay. Left side gets water views, right side gets city on some approaches.
- T+435 Taxi to Intl Terminal. Stay seated until gate. Text home on wheels-stop.
- T+450 Jet bridge, stand, bag down, shoulders roll. Continue in [10-sfo-to-hq](10-sfo-to-hq.md).
