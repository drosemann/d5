# SFO → Meta HQ: Every Step

You are landside at SFO International Terminal, ~13:00 local. Goal:
the Like sign at Bayfront Expy & Willow Rd. Total ~1–1.5 h.

## Step 1 — Regroup (5 min, International Terminal)

- Bathroom, water, SIM check (phone on US data or airport Wi-Fi)
- Open maps, pin **Menlo Park Caltrain station** + hotel
- Decision: **BART + Caltrain** (~$12, ~1 h, recommended) or
  rideshare (~$50–70, ~35 min, if jet lag is winning)

## Step 2 — AirTrain to BART (free, ~5 min)

- Follow signs for **AirTrain** (free monorail, Red or Blue line)
- Ride to **Garage G / BART Station** stop (International Terminal
  G side; from inside the Intl Terminal it's a signed walk instead)
- The BART station sits on the Departures/Ticketing level, G side

## Step 3 — BART ticket + ride to Millbrae (~10 min)

- Buy at the machines or Info Desk (next to the AirTrain escalator):
  a **Clipper card** ($3, reloadable — keep it, it works on BART,
  Caltrain, Muni, SamTrans all trip) or just tap contactless
  bank card / Apple / Google Pay at the gate (no paper tickets anymore)
- Board a **Millbrae-bound** train (2 stops from SFO, ~10 min)
- Get off at **Millbrae** (end of the line for this direction)

## Step 4 — Millbrae → Menlo Park by Caltrain (~25 min)

- Follow **Caltrain** signs inside Millbrae Transit Center
  (connected station, no street crossing)
- Ticket: tap the same Clipper/contactless at the Caltrain reader
  (~$5–7 to Menlo Park) or buy in the Caltrain mobile app
- Board any **southbound** local toward San Jose/Gilroy stopping at
  Menlo Park (every ~30 min; check the departure boards)
- Ride ~25 min; get off at **Menlo Park station** (downtown, very
  walkable — hotel check-in works well here if rooms are ready)

## Step 5 — Menlo Park station → Meta HQ, the last mile (~4 km east)

HQ sits east of US-101 by the bay; Caltrain is on the west side.
Pick one:

1. **Shuttle (cheapest, weekday):** the **M4 Willow Rd shuttle**
   leaves from Menlo Park Caltrain (Willow Rd shuttle stop);
   M3 serves the Marsh Rd side. Free/cheap commuter shuttles —
   check current schedules, they run commute hours.
2. **SamTrans bus 281** toward the Bayfront area, then walk.
3. **Rideshare (~$12–15, ~8–10 min):** the jet-lag choice, door to
   the Like sign at Bayfront Expy & Willow Rd.
4. **Walk:** ~49 min from the station (Moovit figure) — only if you
   want the steps and it's daylight.
5. **Bike:** flat, bikeable, ~15–20 min if the hotel lends one.

## Step 6 — The Like sign + perimeter (2–3 h)

- Photo round: front, both sides, **the Sun-microsystems-logo back**
- Perimeter stroll: MPK20 blue box, tunnel arrival, Main Street side
- Wind down at **Bedwell Bayfront Park** (adjacent, trails + sunset)
- Back downtown for dinner; lights out by ~21:00 local to lock the
  timezone. Day 1 complete — jet lag beaten.

## Day 4 return (reverse, allow 2 h to SFO)

- Menlo Park Caltrain → Millbrae → BART → SFO (~1 h, reverse of
  Steps 4→2; LH455 typically departs ~15:00 from the Intl Terminal,
  so leave Menlo Park by ~12:00)
- SFO check-in: Lufthansa Intl Terminal, 3 h before; ESTA covers
  the return identically; CBP exit is just boarding verification
