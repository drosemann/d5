# Sights: Meta HQ + Silicon Valley

## Meta HQ, 1 Hacker Way (Day 1) — the main event

**Reality check:** no public access, no tours, no lobby. The visit is
the sign and the perimeter — and it is worth it for the story.

- **The Like sign** (Bayfront Expy & Willow Rd): giant thumbs-up,
  the mandatory selfie. Walk around back for the flipped-over
  **Sun Microsystems** logo — Facebook moved into Sun's old campus in
  2011 and kept the monument as a trophy. Best light: late afternoon.
- **MPK20** (Frank Gehry, 2015): 430,000 sq ft, "the largest open floor
  plan in the world" at opening, ~2,800 people in one room, 9-acre
  rooftop park. Visible mass from the street; the tunnel arrival under
  the highway is part of the experience.
- **MPK / Building 16**: the historic half of campus, Main Street
  frontage, employee-buzz perimeter.
- **Bedwell Bayfront Park** (adjacent): bay views, trails, sunset
  picnic — the perfect cooldown after the sign.
- Allow **2–3 hours** including the park. Free.

## Computer History Museum, Mountain View (Day 3)

- 1401 N Shoreline Blvd. **Closed Mon–Tue** — the itinerary starts
  Wednesday so this lands on Friday.
- *Revolution*: 19 galleries, ~1,100 artifacts, the best single
  computing exhibit on Earth. Allow **3+ hours**.
- Pair with the **Google Visitor Experience** (public corner of the
  Googleplex) the same morning — Mountain View covers both.

## Stanford University (Day 3)

- Free 2-hour walking loop: Main Quad, Memorial Church, Hoover Tower
  (viewpoint), Rodin sculpture garden, the Dish hike if legs allow.
- The origin story of the Valley in one campus — HP, Google, and
  countless startups trace back here.

## Apple Park Visitor Center, Cupertino (Day 4)

- The one corporate HQ with a real visitor experience: iPad VR tour
  of the spaceship ring, exhibition, cafe, Apple Store exclusives.
- No campus roaming (same rule as everywhere), but the VR + rooftop
  terrace view of the ring is genuinely good. Allow **2 hours**.

## Quick stops

- **HP garage**, 367 Addison Ave, Palo Alto: "Birthplace of Silicon
  Valley" plaque, photo only, 10 minutes.
- **Intel Museum**, Santa Clara: weekday-only (closed weekends),
  only if the 5th day happens and it's a weekday.
- **Meta MPK16 / Building MPK 16 tour stops**: the organized Day-2
  tours pause here for the narration + photos — same sign, zero walking.
