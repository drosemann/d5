## Pre-requisites from earlier files

- Address: 1 Hacker Way, Menlo Park, CA 94025
- The sign is at Bayfront Expy & Willow Rd, NOT inside the gate
- Campus = historic MPK (ex-Sun, Building 16) + MPK20 (Frank Gehry,
  430,000 sq ft, 9-acre rooftop park, ~2,800 humans in one giant room)
- Back of the Like sign still shows the old Sun Microsystems logo
- Campus is not open to the public — sign + perimeter only, unless invited
