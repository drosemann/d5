# 69 — Field Recordings (Bonus Fun)

The trip as a playlist of sounds. Record them all, hum them later.

## The set

- Caltrain beep: doors closing, middle cars, stand clear.
- Foghorn: Golden Gate, probable, glorious anyway.
- Cable car bell: Powell and Mason, hang on tight.
- Ferry horn: Embarcadero morning, gulls answer.
- Pigeon coo: Menlo Park platform, dawn chorus.
- Wind: Bayfront pullout, jacket required, hat optional casualty.

## How to collect

- One 10-second clip each, phone in chest pocket, no talking.
- Name files by meter: m0021-wind, m0087-beep, m0150-silence.
- Play back on the flight home. Whole pilgrimage in 60 seconds.

- T+35 in [18-makers-pilgrimage](18-makers-pilgrimage.md): video pan, no talking, just wind. It already exists.
