# 55 — Ballad of the Blue Thumb (Bonus Fun)

Sung to any folk tune with three chords. Tip hat out, Clipper cards in.

## Verse 1: Hanau

- Woke at five-thirty, bag was light, S-Bahn humming through the morning gray.
- Z gates are far, but the SkyLine swims, and the thumb is a flight away.

## Chorus

- Oh the blue thumb shines on Bayfront and Willow, Sun behind and Meta ahead.
- Same card taps in and out, power bank full of clout, and we sleep at nine in a Californian bed.

## Verse 2: SFO

- CBP line is long, but the script is short: tourism, four days, Menlo Park.
- BART to Millbrae, Caltrain south, watch Palo Alto for the mark.

## Verse 3: The sign

- Front and flanks and macro bolts, the ghost of Sun upon the back.
- Three eras, one pole, we took the stroll, and the wind filled every crack.

## Verse 4: Home

- Philz in hand, photos backed, forty keepers from two hundred views.
- Ring viewed, garage seen, thumb still blue. Sing it back to Hanau news.

## Encore rule

- If the crowd wants more, play [53-pub-quiz](53-pub-quiz.md) between verses. Losers buy the Mint Mojitos.
