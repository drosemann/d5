# 35 — Budget Per Hour

Mid level reference, per person, from [05-budget](05-budget.md). Re-check week before.

## Flight day

- Home to FRA: S-Bahn ~7.80 euro, coffee 4 euro, gate breakfast 10 to 15 euro.
- Flight: included, plus 6 dollar airport coffee trap avoided by bottle.
- SFO to Menlo Park: BART plus Caltrain ~12 dollars, or rideshare 50 to 70 dollars if wrecked.
- Dinner Santa Cruz Ave: 40 to 60 dollars mid, 15 to 20 tired option.

## Full days

- Breakfast 10 to 15, lunch 15 to 25, dinner 20 to 60. Transit 10 to 20. Museum 25 to 40.
- Tour day: operator fee plus lunch if own. Self-guided: Caltrain day costs plus museum.
- SF extension per [17-extended](17-extended.md): hotel 180 to 250 night, food 75 day, transit 40 total, Alcatraz 45 if booked.

## Totals

- 4-day mid: 1,800 to 2,800 euro all in from Germany.
- 7-day mid: 2,600 to 3,200 euro. Extension 800 to 1,000 euro.
- Buffer: one missed connection per day priced in. Power bank at 100 percent every morning, single point of failure.
