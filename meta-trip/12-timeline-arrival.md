# Minute Timeline: Wheels Up → Like Sign

SFO times local (PDT, CEST−9 in summer / PST, CEST−8 in winter — the
plan uses the −9 summer case; shift +1 h in winter). Landing ~12:41.

## 12:41–13:30 — Deplane → landside (∼50 min)

- 12:41–12:55 Taxi + deplane (wide-body: 10–20 min from touchdown to
  jet bridge to your row moving). Phone on the moment wheels stop —
  US data/eSIM check, "landed" message home.
- 12:55–13:15 **CBP immigration.** Follow "Arrivals", join the
  **ESTA / Visa Waiver / visitors** line. Script: "Tourism. Four
  days. Hotel in Menlo Park. Flying home Saturday." Passport open
  at photo page, return ticket on phone. Photo + fingerprints.
  Wait 15–60 min depending on parallel arrivals — this is the
  biggest variance block of the whole trip.
- 13:15–13:25 **Baggage claim.** Screens show your belt (e.g. CL1).
  Collect, verify tag. Trolley if needed (small fee).
- 13:25–13:30 **Customs** through the green/nothing-to-declare exit.
  Landside. It is ~13:30 local, ~22:30 Hanau time. From here the
  rule is: no sitting still longer than the train rides until 21:00.

## 13:30–13:40 — Regroup (10 min, Intl Terminal)

- 13:30–13:33 Bathroom, water refill, layers check (SFO is often
  windy/cool even when Menlo Park is warm).
- 13:33–13:37 Maps pin check: Menlo Park Caltrain station, hotel,
  Like sign. Screenshot the Caltrain southbound departures.
- 13:37–13:40 Decision gate: transit (default, next steps) vs
  rideshare (only if immigration drained you — ~$50–70, 35 min,
  pickup at Intl Terminal curb, tell driver "1 Hacker Way via
  the Like sign at Bayfront & Willow").

## 13:40–13:50 — AirTrain → BART station (10 min)

- 13:40–13:44 Follow **AirTrain** signs (Red or Blue line both stop
  at Garage G). Ride to **Garage G / BART Station**.
- 13:44–13:50 BART station, Departures/Ticketing level, G side.
  Machines or Info Desk (by the AirTrain escalator): buy **Clipper**
  ($3 card) and load ~$20, or plan contactless tap-in/out with the
  same bank card / phone all trip (simplest — one card, always the
  same one). BART has no paper tickets anymore.

## 13:50–14:05 — BART SFO → Millbrae (15 min)

- 13:50–13:55 Tap in, board a **Millbrae-bound** train (check the
  destination sign; SFO trains also run toward the city — wrong
  direction costs 20 min). 2 stops, ~10 min ride.
- 14:00–14:05 Alight **Millbrae** (terminus this way). Follow
  **Caltrain** transfer signs inside the transit center.

## 14:05–14:35 — Caltrain Millbrae → Menlo Park (30 min)

- 14:05–14:12 Tap Clipper/contactless at the Caltrain reader
  (~$5–7). Check board: any **southbound** stopping at Menlo Park
  (locals stop everywhere; expresses may skip — verify). Headway
  ~30 min worst case; if you just missed one, coffee + the views
  doc re-read on the platform.
- 14:12–14:35 Ride ~25 min. Watch for Palo Alto → **Menlo Park**
  (announced + signed). Stand by the doors after Palo Alto.
- 14:35 Alight Menlo Park. Hotel first if check-in is open
  (drop bag, freshen, 20 min), else bag stays with you.

## 14:35–15:00 — Last mile to the sign (25 min)

- Default: rideshare from the station rank (~$12–15, 8–10 min) to
  **Bayfront Expressway & Willow Rd** (the Like sign, NOT "1 Hacker
  Way" gate — the sign is the photo spot).
- Weekday commute hours alternative: **M4 Willow Rd shuttle** from
  the Caltrain shuttle stop (free/cheap, runs commute windows).
- Say the address out loud to the driver: "the Meta Like sign at
  Bayfront and Willow". Confirm in maps that the pin is the sign.

## 15:00–17:30 — The Makers (2.5 h)

- 15:00–15:30 **The sign.** Front, both sides, thumbs-up selfie,
  wide shot with MPK20 behind, then the back: the flipped
  Sun Microsystems logo. This is the whole pilgrimage — take time.
- 15:30–16:30 Perimeter walk: MPK20 mass + tunnel arrival, Main
  Street frontage, Building 16 side. No entry attempts (security
  will turn you around politely — don't be that tourist).
- 16:30–17:30 **Bedwell Bayfront Park**: trails, bay views, sit down
  for the first time today. Snack + water. "Greetings from the makers"
  message home with the sign photo.
- 17:30 Rideshare/bus back downtown. Dinner on Santa Cruz Ave.
  In bed by ~21:00 local. Jet lag: beaten (see flight doc).
